# flash-sale: passed at 200 users/s

> many shows on sale (45 x 1000 seats)

- Date: 2026-11-03T01:43:40.048Z
- Commit: 1467abc
- Test: Advance booking opens for 45 1,000-seat shows at once (enough that seats outlast the event) and new users arrive at a fixed rate (Users/s). Each user wants one show, picked by popularity (Zipf: a few shows get most users), and loads its seat map (layout through the CDN edge, availability from the app on every attempt); if it is sold out they look at another show, up to 3. They want 1-4 seats of the type at their preferred spot (most want the middle-back rows near the centre, some recliners, some an aisle, a few the front), think 0-3 s and ask `POST /bookings/best-available`; on a 409 they try again with the other seat type, up to 3 bookings. Runs once, after warming up the JVM with `mixed`. **p95 seat map** is the availability request.
